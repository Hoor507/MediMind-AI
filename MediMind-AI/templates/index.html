How MediMind works

A transparent workflow designed for a university demonstration.

01
⌕

Collect context

Your symptom description and optional context are sent securely from the local backend to the AI provider.

02
◈

Structure the response

The model is asked for possible explanations, uncertainties, warning signs, and next steps in a structured format.

03
✚

Apply safety checks

The backend checks for selected critical warning phrases and can override the model with an emergency alert.

Important limitation: keyword checks can miss emergencies and can also flag symptoms that need context. This is not a clinically validated medical device.
